# CAR SALES REPORT- POWERBI
## Introduction
This project presents a Car Sales Report built through end‑to‑end analysis, starting with data cleaning in Excel to standardize car model details, remove duplicates, and handle missing values using formulas such as VLOOKUP, SUMIFS, and logical imputation. The cleaned dataset was then imported into Power BI, where interactive dashboards and visual reports were designed to highlight sales trends, top‑selling models, revenue patterns, and regional performance. By combining Excel’s preprocessing with Power BI’s visualization capabilities, the project delivers clear insights into car sales dynamics and supports data‑driven decision‑making.

## Data Preparation in Excel
### Data Cleaning  
Standardized car model names to avoid duplicates caused by inconsistent spelling or formatting.

### Duplicate Removal  
Ensured accuracy by eliminating repeated entries that could inflate sales figures.

### Handling Missing Values  
Applied logical imputation and formulas like VLOOKUP and SUMIFS to fill gaps in sales records.

### Formula Integration  
Used conditional logic to validate data consistency before importing into Power BI.

## Power BI Dashboard & Visualization
### Sales Trends  
Line charts revealed seasonal peaks (e.g., festive months) and dips in off-seasons.

### Top-Selling Models  
Bar charts highlighted which car models consistently drive revenue.

### Revenue Patterns  
KPI cards tracked total revenue, average sales per model, and year-over-year growth.

### Regional Performance  
Map visuals showed geographic hotspots, identifying regions with strong or weak sales.

## Key Insights
### Model Popularity  
A small set of models contributed disproportionately to overall sales.

### Regional Variations  
Urban regions favored compact cars, while rural areas leaned toward SUVs and trucks.

### Seasonality  
Sales surged during holidays and promotional campaigns, suggesting strong responsiveness to marketing.

### Revenue Concentration  
A few high-priced models generated significant revenue despite lower unit sales.

##  Recommendations
### Inventory Optimization  
Stock more of the top-selling models in high-demand regions to reduce shortages.

### Targeted Marketing  
Launch promotions around festive seasons and tailor campaigns to regional preferences.

### Product Strategy  
Consider expanding SUV offerings in rural markets and compact cars in urban centers.

### Revenue Diversification  
Balance reliance on a few high-revenue models by promoting mid-range vehicles.

### Customer Insights  
Use Power BI’s drill-down features to analyze customer demographics and buying behavior for personalized offers.

## Strategic Impact
By combining Excel preprocessing with Power BI dashboards, the project delivers a robust end-to-end reporting solution. It empowers sales managers to make data-driven decisions on inventory, marketing, and product strategy, ultimately boosting profitability and customer satisfaction.
